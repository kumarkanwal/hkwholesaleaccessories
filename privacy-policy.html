Privacy Policy

HK Wholesale Accessories Pty Ltd (ABN 45 696 536 400, ACN 696 536 400) respects your privacy and is committed to responsible handling of personal information.

Last updated: November 2026

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information. It is based on the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles to the extent they apply to our activities.

Personal Information We Collect

We may collect your name, business or trading name, Australian Business Number (ABN), email address, phone number, business or delivery address, and details contained in an enquiry, order or trade account application.

How We Collect Information

We collect information when you submit a website contact form or trade account application, contact us by email or phone, place or discuss an order, or otherwise communicate with us. We may also receive information from an authorised representative of your business.

Why We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, assess and process trade account applications, administer customer accounts, process and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support, keep business records, and meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Disclosure to Third Parties

We may disclose relevant information to couriers and logistics providers, payment providers, professional advisers, and IT, website or hosting providers where reasonably required for our business. We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law.

We do not sell personal information.

Overseas Disclosure

Some technology or hosting providers may store or process data outside Australia. The countries involved depend on the providers and systems used and may change over time. Where required, we take reasonable steps in relation to overseas disclosures of personal information.

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable administrative, technical and physical steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to maintain appropriate business records, or to meet legal, accounting and compliance requirements. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it where lawful and practicable.

Access and Correction

You may ask for access to personal information we hold about you or request that inaccurate, out-of-date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading information be corrected. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and may refuse a request where permitted by law, in which case we will explain the available complaint options.

Marketing and Opt-Out

Where permitted, we may send business customers information about products, availability or trade services. Commercial electronic messages will be managed in line with the Spam Act 2003. You may opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe method in a message or by contacting us. We will process opt-out requests within the period required by law.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and basic analytics to support website functionality and understand general usage, such as pages visited, browser type, device information and approximate location derived from an IP address. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some website features.

Privacy Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, please contact us in writing and provide enough detail for us to investigate. We will acknowledge and respond to your complaint within a reasonable period.

If you are not satisfied with our response, or we do not respond within 30 days, you may be able to make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) through www.oaic.gov.au.
