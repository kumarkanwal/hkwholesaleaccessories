Wholesale Trade Enquiries Only

This website is for wholesale trade enquiries only and is not a retail store. No public online checkout or consumer retail purchasing is offered through this website.

Approved Australian Businesses

We supply only registered Australian businesses and authorised retailers that we approve. A valid Australian Business Number (ABN) is required. We may request business, identity, licensing or other information before approving an account or order.

Age Requirement

Website visitors, account applicants and customers must be 18 years of age or older. By making an enquiry, applying for an account or placing an order, you confirm that you meet this requirement and are authorised to act for the relevant business.

Customer Legal and Licensing Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for understanding and complying with all Commonwealth, state and territory laws, regulations, licensing requirements and local rules that apply to their business and to the possession, storage, transport, display, advertising, sale and supply of products.

This includes any restrictions that apply to smoking accessories, lighters, butane or other gas products, and nitrous oxide cream chargers. Product availability does not represent advice that a product may lawfully be sold in every jurisdiction or by every business.

Cream Chargers

Cream chargers are supplied for legitimate food preparation and culinary use only. They must not be misused, supplied for inhalation, or sold to any person where the customer knows or reasonably suspects they will be used unlawfully or for inhalation. Customers must maintain appropriate sales, age-verification, staff training, storage and compliance practices required by applicable law.

Refusal or Cancellation

We may refuse or cancel an application, account or order at our discretion, including where information cannot be verified, legal or safety requirements may not be met, payment terms are not satisfied, or we believe products may be acquired, supplied or resold unlawfully. Any refund due following cancellation will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the agreed payment arrangements.

Product availability, brands, packaging, specifications, quantities and supply timeframes are subject to confirmation. Images and descriptions are catalogue references and actual products may vary. Please confirm current information before ordering.
